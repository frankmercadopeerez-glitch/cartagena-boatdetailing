The Service

In-Water Hull Cleaning by a Professional Dive Team

Warm Caribbean water and salinity encourage marine growth such as algae, barnacles and biofilm. This fouling increases drag, can reduce performance and may shorten the useful life of an antifouling system when it is not managed.

Our in-water hull cleaning service uses a professional dive team and tools selected for the hull material, coating and level of growth. Work is performed at the berth or anchorage when access and conditions are safe, so a haul-out is not normally required.

  • Cleaning of the keel, centerboard and rudder
  • Removal of algae, barnacles and marine growth
  • Cleaning below the waterline
  • Visual review of the submerged hull and anodes
  • Propeller, shaft and stern-gear cleaning when accessible
  • Post-service photo report

Why It Matters

Marine Growth in the Colombian Caribbean

Warm Caribbean water and salinity can accelerate marine growth. The actual rate varies with location, vessel use, season and the antifouling system. A fouled hull can:

  • Increase drag and fuel consumption
  • Reduce speed and propulsion efficiency
  • Stress or shorten the life of antifouling coatings
  • Hide corrosion or damage below the waterline
  • Carry marine organisms between operating areas

Frequently Asked Questions

What Boat Owners Ask Most

Does the boat need to be hauled out for cleaning?
Normally, no. The submerged hull is cleaned while the vessel remains at its berth or anchorage, once safe access and working conditions have been confirmed.
How long does an underwater hull cleaning take?
Timing depends on vessel size, hull design, the amount and type of marine growth, access and water conditions. We confirm the expected duration after reviewing the vessel.
Can underwater cleaning damage antifouling paint?
Aggressive or unsuitable cleaning can damage a coating. We inspect its visible condition and select tools and techniques for the antifouling type. If the coating appears fragile or compromised, we explain the risk before proceeding.
Do you serve Cartagena and nearby areas?
We cover Cartagena de Indias, private docks and selected nearby anchorages. Contact us with your location so we can confirm travel, marina access, sea conditions and availability.
What types of vessels do you service?
We assess sailboats, motor yachts, catamarans, powerboats and workboats. The method is selected for fiberglass (GRP), aluminum or steel hulls and the condition of their coating.
