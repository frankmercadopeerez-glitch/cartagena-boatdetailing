What Is Marine Ceramic Coating?

Ceramic coating is a liquid protection system applied to a properly cleaned and prepared surface. Once cured according to the product instructions, it adds hydrophobic behavior and can make routine salt and dirt removal easier.

  • Hydrophobic Finish

    Helps water bead and reduces the adhesion of salt and common surface contaminants.

  • Surface Protection

    Adds a sacrificial coating layer, but does not make the surface scratch-proof or replace impact protection.

  • Product-Specific Bonding

    Preparation, cure time and compatibility are defined for the selected product and existing finish.

Easier Decontamination

A compatible coating can make routine salt and grime removal easier. Product limits and approved cleaners are stated in the care instructions.

Gloss and Finish

Coating can enhance the gloss of a well-prepared surface. The final result depends primarily on the condition and correction of the underlying paint or gelcoat.

Practical Maintenance

Hydrophobic behavior can reduce contaminant adhesion, while regular gentle washing and periodic inspection remain necessary in Cartagena's marine environment.

Application Process

1

Technical Wash and Decontamination

Surface Correction When Required

Frequently Asked Questions

What to Know Before Coating Your Boat

How long does marine ceramic coating last in Cartagena?
Expected durability depends on the selected product, surface preparation, exposure, washing practices and maintenance. We state the product and applicable coverage in the written quote instead of promising one fixed lifespan for every vessel.
Does ceramic coating replace gelcoat polishing?
No. Polishing corrects oxidation and surface defects when the gelcoat condition allows it. Ceramic coating protects the prepared finish; it does not repair damaged or severely deteriorated gelcoat.
Does ceramic coating help protect against salt and Caribbean sun exposure?
A suitable coating can add a hydrophobic, easier-to-clean barrier that helps reduce contaminant adhesion. It does not make the surface maintenance-free or eliminate the need for regular salt removal and inspection.
How long does ceramic coating application take on a yacht?
Timing depends on vessel size, access, surface condition, correction work, the selected product and its curing requirements. We confirm the estimated schedule after inspection.
Does marine ceramic coating require special maintenance?
Yes. Regular salt removal with compatible products, gentle wash methods and periodic inspection help preserve performance. The maintenance plan follows the selected product's written instructions.
Is ceramic coating compatible with PPF?
Some coatings are compatible with specific paint-protection films, but compatibility must be checked with both product manufacturers before application. We define the approved combination in the quote.
