What PPF Protects Against

  • Physical impacts: dock lines, debris, bird strikes
  • Abrasion: dock edges, anchor chains, fender marks
  • UV degradation: blocks Caribbean UV from reaching paint
  • Salt corrosion: waterproof barrier against salt crystal penetration
  • Self-healing: heat-activated surface repairs minor swirls and light scratches
  • Chemical staining: fuel, oil, bird droppings, barnacle residue

Frequently Asked Questions

What is marine PPF and how is it different from ceramic coating?

PPF is a 150–200 micron thermoplastic polyurethane film that protects against impacts, scratches and physical abrasion with self-healing capability. Ceramic coating is a chemical hydrophobic layer without physical thickness. They are complementary: PPF for mechanical protection, ceramic for chemical protection.

How long does PPF last on a boat in Cartagena?

High-quality marine-grade PPF has a guaranteed lifespan of 5 to 10 years. Professional installation with premium PPF can be guaranteed for 7+ years without yellowing, bubbling or delamination in Caribbean conditions.

Does PPF change the color or finish of the paint?

A quality PPF correctly installed is virtually invisible. Optical clarity of premium films exceeds 95%, without altering color or paint depth. Available in gloss or matte finish to match the vessel's existing paint.

Can PPF be applied to any surface of the yacht?

PPF adapts to complex curved surfaces. Compatible with fiberglass, gelcoat, aluminum, steel and composite materials. The most common zones are bow, gunwales, waterline, roof and cabin.

How is PPF removed at end of life?

Correctly installed PPF is removed without damaging the paint or gelcoat. The process involves heating the film with a heat gun and peeling it in strips, then removing adhesive residue with isopropanol.
