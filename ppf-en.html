What Is PPF?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a clear polyurethane film applied to compatible painted or gelcoat surfaces on your boat.

Application Process

1

Inspection and Technical Cleaning

We inspect the finish, previous repairs and edges in the requested area. Technical cleaning removes salt, residue and other contamination that could interfere with adhesion.

Timing: confirmed after the initial inspection

2

Surface Preparation

We decontaminate the surface and identify defects that must be corrected before installation. Adhesion promoter is used only when the product and the geometry require it.

Scope: preparation is itemized in the quote

3

PPF Installation

The selected film is positioned on the agreed areas, with careful alignment, edge placement and controlled drying. We review bubbles, seams and transitions before delivery.

Timing: depends on geometry and coverage

4

Edge Review and Final Inspection

When compatible with the selected system, we apply the recommended finishing protection. We document the installed product, areas, edges and applicable coverage.

Delivery: after the required setting period

5

Care Instructions

We explain safe washing, compatible products, edge care and recommended inspections. The installation record identifies the film and the written conditions that apply.

Included: product-specific care guidance

Important: The required setting period and the earliest safe time for washing or operating the boat depend on the selected film, installed area and environmental conditions. We confirm those instructions at delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

What to Know Before Choosing PPF

What is marine PPF, and how is it different from ceramic coating?
PPF (Paint Protection Film) is a clear polyurethane film applied to painted or gelcoat surfaces. It helps reduce physical wear from light scratches, rubbing and abrasion in installed areas. Ceramic coating is a liquid, hydrophobic coating that helps with chemical contamination and cleaning; it does not provide the same physical film barrier. The two systems can be complementary when the selected products are compatible.
How long does PPF last on a boat in Cartagena?
Service life depends on the selected film, surface preparation, installed area, UV exposure, use and maintenance. Before installation, the written quote identifies the product, the manufacturer's available coverage and the conditions that apply to our installation.
Does PPF change the color or finish of a yacht?
Clear PPF is designed to preserve the appearance of the underlying finish, but the final look depends on the film, the existing surface and edge placement. Gloss and matte options may be available. We review a sample and the planned seams before installation.
Can PPF be applied to any yacht surface?
Not every surface is suitable. We inspect adhesion, finish condition, curvature, previous repairs and coatings before approving an area. Smooth painted or gelcoat surfaces are common candidates; textured, deteriorated or unstable finishes may require another solution.
How is PPF removed at the end of its service life?
Removal should be performed with controlled heat and suitable adhesive-removal products. The condition and age of the film and the underlying finish affect the process, so we inspect the surface before confirming the removal method.
Which yacht areas benefit most from PPF?
PPF is commonly considered for high-contact zones such as the bow, gunwales, boarding areas, hatch surrounds and other areas exposed to ropes or dock contact. The recommended coverage is defined after inspecting how the boat is used and the condition of its finish.
